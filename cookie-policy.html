Cookie policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Does this website use cookies?

This website does not set its own cookies, nor any analytics or advertising cookies. It only stores a few technical preferences in your browser so that it works as you expect. These preferences are not sent to any server and are exempt from consent under Article 22.2 of Spanish Law LSSI-CE.

2. Technical storage in your browser

NamePurposeDuration

3. Third-party cookies: Google Maps

The map in the "Visit us" section does not load automatically. Only when you press "Show map" does Google Maps (Google Ireland Limited) connect from your browser, and it may set its own cookies. You can read how Google uses them in Google's cookie policy.
